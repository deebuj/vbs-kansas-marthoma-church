What the sessions cover

Three teaching sessions, one question at the centre of each.

Session 1 · Friday

Who is Jesus?

Jesus is God's promised Son — the Messiah people had waited for. From Matthew 16:13-20.

Session 2 · Saturday AM

Was Jesus just an ordinary man?

Jesus is God's perfect Son, who came to be the Saviour of the world. From the baptism of Jesus.

Session 3 · Saturday PM

Was Jesus just a teacher?

Everything Jesus said and did showed He is the powerful Son of God. From Matthew 15:29-39.
